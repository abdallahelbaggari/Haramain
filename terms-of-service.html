7. The transfer rules

Transfers are handled only by software following these fixed rules, applied the same way to everyone. No person at Haramain reviews, approves, holds, freezes, reverses or decides on any individual transfer or account.

  • Before you pay, a transfer is accepted only if: the recipient has signed in to Haramain with Pi; you are not sending to yourself; the amount is at least 0.01 π and within the per-transfer limit; and it fits within the daily limits per person and the app's overall daily limit.   • Pausing: if Pi Network's rules, the law or a technical fault require it, new transfers may be paused for all users at once; the app shows this. A pause never applies to one person, and transfers already paid are still delivered or refunded automatically under these rules.
