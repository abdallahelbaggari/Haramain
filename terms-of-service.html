7Fees

All Haramain tools are free. Each transfer sent within Haramain carries a network fee of 0.01 π, displayed before you approve. The Supporter Pass (1 π) is optional; it supports the development of Haramain, adds a Supporter badge, unlocks no other feature and carries no reward or financial return.

8How transfers work

When you send Pi within Haramain, you approve the amount plus the 0.01 π network fee through Pi Network's user-to-app payment flow. Once Pi Network confirms the payment, Haramain's systems deliver the full amount to the recipient automatically: to a @username through Pi Network's app-to-user payment flow, or to a wallet address as a payment on the Pi blockchain. Funds pass through Haramain's app wallet only for the time settlement takes, normally a few seconds.

Every transfer ends in one of two outcomes, both recorded on the Pi blockchain: delivered to the recipient, or refunded to you. Before you approve, Haramain shows the recipient, amount, fee and total, and alerts you when paying someone for the first time.

9Transfer rules

Transfers are processed by software under fixed rules applied equally to every user. No individual at Haramain reviews, holds, freezes or reverses a transfer.

  • Before payment — a transfer is accepted only if the recipient is a Haramain user or a valid Pi wallet address with an active account, you are not paying yourself, the amount is at least 0.01 π and within any limits published in the app, and Haramain can settle it at that moment. Otherwise it is cancelled and you are not charged.
  • Delivery — the transfer is delivered once Pi Network confirms your payment. The Pi blockchain is checked before any retry, so a transfer is never paid twice.
  • Automatic refund — if the recipient can no longer receive, or delivery fails after repeated attempts over at least one hour, the full amount is refunded to your Pi Wallet. The 0.01 π network fee is not returned.
  • Finality — a delivered transfer is final and cannot be reversed by Haramain, the sender or the recipient.
  • Suspension — where Pi Network's rules, the law or a technical fault require it, new transfers may be paused for all users. Transfers already paid are still delivered or refunded.

Changes to these rules apply only to new transfers and are published in the app and in these Terms.
