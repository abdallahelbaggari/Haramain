Summary

  • Most of what you keep in Haramain stays on your device.
  • Our servers process only what payments, transfers and shared features require, and only for as long as needed.
  • Payments and transfers are processed automatically and recorded on the public Pi blockchain.
  • We never sell data, never use advertising trackers and never access your passphrase or private keys.
  • You may request a copy or deletion of your data at any time.

1Who we are

Haramain (haramain.pi) is operated by ELBAGGARI INVESTMENT LIMITED, the controller of personal data processed by Haramain's servers. Contact: support.haramain@gmail.com.

2Information kept on your device

The following are stored on your device and are not sent to us: checklists, Tawaf and Sa'i counts, dhikr and adhkar progress, prayer log, goals and badges, Quran bookmarks and reading position, water log, health card, medications, emergency contacts, notes, documents and photos you add, budget, itinerary, saved receipts, contacts and favourites, the name on your receipts and your settings. Clearing Haramain's data in the Pi Browser removes them. Keep your device locked, as anyone who can open it may see them.

4The Pi blockchain

Payments, deliveries and refunds are recorded on the public Pi blockchain, where wallet addresses, amounts and times are visible to anyone and cannot be deleted by us or by you. The narration you enter is shown to the recipient and kept in our transfer record; it is not written to the blockchain.

5Location

Location is used only when you allow it and use a feature that needs it: prayer times and Qibla (approximate coordinates are sent to a prayer-time service), the live map, family check-ins and emergency messages you choose to send. Shared live location is deleted when you stop sharing, and at the latest after 12 hours.

6Service providers

  • Pi Network — authentication, payments, app-to-user payouts and the Pi blockchain.
  • Cloudflare — hosting, server functions and databases.
  • AI providers — responses for the assistant and translator; only the text you type is sent.
  • Prayer-time service — approximate coordinates for prayer times and Qibla.
  • Public data services — weather, Quran text and recitation, maps and market rates; no personal data is shared.

7What we never do

  • Sell, rent or trade personal data.
  • Use advertising trackers or build advertising profiles.
  • Request or store your passphrase, seed phrase or private key.
  • Upload your passport, documents or health card to our servers.
  • Move Pi from your wallet; only you can, by approving a payment through Pi Network.

8How we protect information

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) with strict browser security policies.
  • Secret keys held only on our servers as protected secrets.
  • Every payment verified with Pi Network before it is accepted.
  • Blockchain checks before any retry, so nothing is paid twice.
  • Shared features protected with random access codes and automatic deletion.

No system is completely secure. If an incident affects your data, we will inform affected users in the app and act promptly to contain it.

10Your rights

You may request a copy of the data our servers hold about you, its correction or its deletion by writing to support.haramain@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days. Records on the public Pi blockchain cannot be deleted. Depending on where you live, data protection laws such as Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law or the EU GDPR may give you further rights, including the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

11Children

Haramain is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Payments and transfers are available only to adults. If you believe a child has shared data with us, contact us and we will delete it.

12International processing

Our servers run on Cloudflare's global network, so data may be processed outside your country under industry-standard safeguards.

13Changes and contact

We update this policy when Haramain changes how it uses data and revise the effective date above. Material changes are announced in the app.
