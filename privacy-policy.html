Privacy Policy

In short: most of what you keep in Haramain stays only on your phone. We use our server only for the features that need it (payments and transfers, shared live location, family groups, the Lost & Found board and the assistant), we keep that data only as long as needed, we never sell data, we never show ads that track you, and we never see your Pi passphrase or private keys.

1. Who we are

Haramain (haramain.pi) is an independent Hajj and Umrah companion app for the Pi Browser, operated by its developer. Contact: support.haramain@gmail.com. Haramain is not run by Pi Network or by any government.

2. Kept only on your phone

These stay in your browser's storage on your device. We do not receive them: your checklist, Tawaf and Sa'i counts, dhikr and adhkar progress, prayer log, daily goals and badges, Quran bookmarks and reading position, water log, health card, medications, emergency contacts, notes, documents and photos you add, memories, budget, itinerary, saved payment receipts, your full name for receipts, and your settings. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the Pi Browser deletes them. Because they live on your device, anyone who can unlock your phone may see them, so keep your phone locked.

4. Pi sign-in and payments

When you sign in with Pi, the Pi app shares your Pi username and a user ID with Haramain. Payments are made in Pi's own payment window. Our server checks each payment with Pi Network (amount, status and transaction ID) before approving and completing it. We never see your passphrase, seed phrase, private keys or wallet balance.

5. Sending Pi to Haramain users

If you send Pi to another Haramain user, you pay Haramain in Pi's payment window and Haramain's app wallet then pays the recipient through Pi Network's official app-to-user payments. To do this, we keep the transfer record listed in section 3. Transfers are written to the public Pi blockchain, where wallet addresses, amounts, memos and times are visible to anyone and cannot be deleted by us or by you.

6. Location

Location is used only when you allow it in your browser and use a feature that needs it: prayer times and the Qibla direction (your approximate coordinates are sent to the AlAdhan prayer-time service), your position on the live map, family check-ins and emergency messages you choose to send. When you share live location, it is deleted from our server as soon as you tap Stop, and at the latest after 12 hours. Directions open your own maps app.

7. Services we use

  • Pi Network: sign-in, payments and app-to-user payouts.
  • Cloudflare: hosting, server functions and the database for shared features.
  • Google Gemini (or Anthropic as a backup): answers for the assistant and translator. We send only the text you type.
  • Public data services, which receive no personal data from us: AlAdhan (prayer times), Open-Meteo (weather), AlQuran Cloud and EveryAyah (Quran text and recitation), OKX and CoinGecko (Pi price), open.er-api.com (currency rates), GDACS (alerts), OpenStreetMap and CARTO (maps), Pi blockchain explorers (receipt checks), and Google Fonts, cdnjs and jsDelivr (fonts and code libraries).

Each service handles requests under its own privacy policy.

8. What we never do

  • We never sell, rent or trade personal data.
  • We do not use advertising trackers or build advertising profiles.
  • We never ask for or store your Pi passphrase, seed phrase or private keys.
  • We do not upload your passport, documents or health card to our servers.
  • We do not read your contacts, messages or files outside Haramain.

9. How we protect data

  • All connections use HTTPS, and the app sends standard browser security headers and a content policy that blocks scripts from unknown sites.
  • Secret keys live only on our server and are never sent to your phone.
  • Payments and payouts are checked with Pi Network before anything is approved or paid, and each payout is made only once. If a payment cannot be checked, it is not approved.
  • Transfers have per-transfer and daily limits and an overall daily cap, accounts involved in fraud can be put on hold, and the app warns you before paying someone for the first time.
  • Shared features use random secret codes, sign-in checks with Pi, member-only access and automatic deletion.
  • Receipts can be checked against the public blockchain so altered receipts can be detected.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach affecting your data, we will inform affected users in the app and act to contain it.

11. Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything on your phone by clearing Haramain's data in the Pi Browser.
  • Stop live sharing, leave a family group or mark your notice resolved at any time.
  • Write to support.haramain@gmail.com to ask for a copy of the data our server holds about you, to correct it, or to delete it. We reply within 30 days. Records on the public Pi blockchain cannot be deleted.
  • You can stop using Haramain at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example under Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law, the EU's GDPR or Nigeria's Data Protection Act), you may have further rights, and you can complain to your data protection authority.

12. Children

Haramain is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Payments and transfers are only for users who are adults where they live. If you believe a child has shared data with us, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Where data is processed

Our server runs on Cloudflare's global network, so data may be processed in countries other than yours. We use providers that protect data with industry-standard security.

14. Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app changes how it uses data, and change the date at the top. Important changes are announced in the app. Questions: support.haramain@gmail.com.
