Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026 (shared features added)

Saved only on your phone

Your checklist, Tawaf and Sa'i counts, dhikr, prayer log, water log, health card, medications, emergency contacts, notes, documents, memories, budget and settings are saved in your browser's storage on this device. We do not receive them. Clearing the app's data deletes them.

Pi sign-in

When you sign in with Pi, the Pi app shares your Pi username and a user ID with Haramain. We never see your passphrase, seed phrase or private keys.

Payments

The optional Supporter Pass is a Pi payment made in the Pi app. Our server checks the payment with Pi Network (amount, status and transaction ID) before approving and completing it.

Location

If you allow location, your position is used to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction (your coordinates are sent to the AlAdhan prayer-time service), to show your zone in the tracker, and to add a map link when you choose to share a check-in or emergency message. We do not store your location on a server.

Live location sharing

If you tap “Share live with family”, your position is sent to our server and shown to anyone who has your link, until you tap Stop or for at most 12 hours. When sharing ends, the location is deleted from our server.

Family groups

If you create or join a family group, the name you enter, your check-ins and (when you choose to share it) your last location are stored on our server and shown only to members of that group. Members who are inactive for 7 days are removed automatically, and you can leave a group at any time.

Lost & Found board

Notices you post are public to all Haramain users, together with your Pi username and any contact you choose to add. Notices are removed after 30 days, when you mark them resolved, or when several users report them.

Questions and translations

Text you type in Ask or Translate is sent through our server to an AI service to produce the answer. Please do not include personal details. We do not keep these messages.

Public information

The app loads public data such as Makkah weather, Quran text and the Pi price. No personal data is sent with these requests. Directions open your own maps app.

What we do not do

  • No advertising trackers and no selling of data.
  • No collection of passport numbers or identity documents on our servers.

Changes

We will update this page if the app changes how it uses data.
