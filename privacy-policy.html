Privacy Policy
Effective Date: June 2026
Welcome to HARAMAIN — The Web3 Pilgrimage Platform. HARAMAIN is a pilgrimage support and utility application designed to provide tools, information, guidance, and digital experiences for pilgrims worldwide. Your privacy matters to us.

1. Who We Are

HARAMAIN is an independent application project available on Pi Network at haramain.pi.

HARAMAIN is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to any government, religious authority, organization, institution, or the Pi Network Core Team unless explicitly stated.

2. Information We Collect

We are committed to data minimization. HARAMAIN does not intentionally request or collect:

  • One-Time Passwords (OTP)
  • Passwords or recovery phrases
  • Private keys or wallet credentials
  • Banking or sensitive payment credentials
  • Identity verification documents unless clearly required and disclosed

Information that may be processed to operate app features:

  • Basic app usage information
  • Device and browser information
  • Language preferences and feature settings
  • Transaction references required for supported payment features
  • User-generated content voluntarily submitted

3. Pi Payment Integration

Where Pi payment functionality is available:

  • Payment processing follows official Pi Network payment mechanisms
  • HARAMAIN does not store private wallet credentials
  • HARAMAIN only processes transaction references necessary to support payment functionality
Users remain responsible for reviewing and confirming all transactions before approval.

4. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and deliver app features
  • Improve reliability and performance
  • Provide user support
  • Prevent abuse and fraud
  • Improve user experience
We do not sell user information to any third party.

5. Data Storage

We aim to store the minimum information necessary to operate the platform. Some data may be temporarily processed to enable functionality and improve reliability. User preferences and progress are stored locally on your device.

6. Security

We implement reasonable technical and operational measures to protect data. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Users should:

  • Protect their Pi Network accounts
  • Verify payment details before confirming
  • Avoid sharing sensitive credentials with any service

7. Third-Party Services

Some features may rely on external services, including AI services, prayer time APIs, and weather data providers. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. HARAMAIN is not responsible for third-party data practices.

8. Children's Privacy

HARAMAIN is not intended for users where local law restricts access based on age. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Continued use of HARAMAIN means acceptance of the updated terms. The effective date at the top indicates the most recent revision.
